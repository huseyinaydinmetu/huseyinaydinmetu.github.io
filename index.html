Short Bio

Hüseyin Aydın [hyseˈjin ˈajdɯn] — I'm an assistant professor at the Department of Computer Engineering, in METU. I graduated from the same department in 2015. After graduation, I worked there as a Teaching and Research Assitant, during which I obtained my MSc degree in 2017 and subsequently completed my PhD studies in 2022. I was a post-doc researcher in the Resposible AI group at Utrecht University between 2023 and 2025.

Research Interests

  • Privacy
  • Responsible AI
